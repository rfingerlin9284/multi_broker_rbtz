STRATEGY-FIRST APPROACH
------------------------

1. **Phase 1: Local Strategy Scan (Always Runs)**
   - Cost: $0.00 (runs locally)
   - Speed: Fast (~1-2 seconds)
   - Scans: ALL symbols in universe
   - Output: Strategy signals with confidence scores
   - ✅ FABIO RSI 40 threshold PERMANENT here

2. **Phase 2: Selective AI Hive Validation (Conditional)**
   - Cost: $0.01-0.02 per call
   - Speed: Slower (API latency)
   - Scans: ONLY symbols with strong strategy signals
   - Condition: Budget allows AND strategy confidence >= 60%
   - Output: AI agent confirmations/rejections

KEY INSIGHT: Strategy scan generates 167 trades with RSI 40 threshold.
             AI Hive validates only the best candidates (~5-10 symbols).
             Cost: $10/day maximum vs $500+ without control.
