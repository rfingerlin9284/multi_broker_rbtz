### WITH AI ($350/week cost):

**Wait, that's LESS net profit!**

- Higher win rate (58% vs 50%)
- Fewer losing trades to manage
- Better risk management

### REAL ADVANTAGE: Quality over Quantity

**Without AI:** 167 trades/week = 24 trades/day (exhausting!)
**With AI:** 84 trades/week = 12 trades/day (manageable)
