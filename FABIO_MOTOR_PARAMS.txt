KEY INSIGHT
-----------
The original RSI threshold of 50 was filtering out legitimate 
bullish momentum setups. RSI 40-50 range often indicates 
"neutral-to-bullish" conditions rather than weakness, especially 
when fast EMA is above slow EMA and price is trending up.

Lowering to 40 allows entry into momentum setups earlier in the 
move, before RSI reaches overbought (70+), while still avoiding 
oversold (<40) bearish conditions.
